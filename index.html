Winger.

Two decision drills, played from a winger's own eye level. Pick a side, watch a moment of live play, then choose. All four track the left and right wing separately, so a weaker side shows up on its own.

ShoulderFirst touch

The ball is already on its way to you. Half a second to look up the pitch over your shoulder, then decide where the first touch goes — down the line, 45° in, square inside, or set it back.

PocketMovement

The ball is still at your midfielder's feet and you are pinned high and wide. Two seconds to read the picture, then decide where to move before the pass comes — high and wide, make a run, pinch in to the pocket, or drop and square.

Two-second watch 16 reps Left or right
Start
BodyReceiving

The same picture as the first-touch drill, asking a different question: can you turn, or not. One call — open body or closed — judged on where your marker will be when the ball actually arrives.

Half-second look 16 reps Left or right
Start
BylineCrossing

You are at the byline with the ball and three runners attacking the box. One glance in, then pick the delivery — near post, cutback, far post, or nobody is on and you keep it.
