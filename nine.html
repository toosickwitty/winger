Which role today

Mode

16 reps, about five minutes. You are the centre-forward of a 4-3-3, and each rep tells you which one: the striker or the false 9. Spanish positional play (juego de posición) cuts the pitch into five lanes and puts players in different lanes and at different heights: no more than two team-mates in a lane, no more than three on a line. A few seconds of play, then it freezes as the last pass is struck: tap where you should be, and run there.

We have it, as the striker — on their last line, in the middle, up against their centre-backs: pin them (fijar a los centrales) and stay onside for the run in behind.
We have it, as the false 9 — come off their centre-backs into the gap between their midfield and back line, in the middle (falso nueve). Your wingers start wide, ready to run in behind if a centre-back follows you.
They have it — you are the front of the block, the highest man in the middle: in our block, screen the pass into their 6 (tapar al pivote); when we press high, press the centre-back who gets it.
We just lost it — the ball near you: press it. Away from you: come back and add a man in midfield (presión tras pérdida).

Learn paints the lanes on the grass and shows everyone on the map when you tap. Test takes all of that away.
